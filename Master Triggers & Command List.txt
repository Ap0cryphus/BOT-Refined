======================================================================
 KAEKAE BOT - MASTER TRIGGERS & COMMAND LIST
======================================================================

[Audio & Listening Controls]
  !listen              - Turn microphone listening ON
  !mute                - Turn microphone listening OFF
  !transcribe          - Echo transcribed room speech to Camfrog chat
  !transcribed         - Stop echoing transcribed speech to chat
  !pending             - Check how many un-transcribed audio clips are queued
  !catchup             - Transcribe all queued/pending speech clips now

[Chat Temperament & Banter]
  !chat                - Turn chatty mode ON (KaeKae mingles in chat)
  !chat off            - Turn chatty mode OFF (Quiet in room)
  !calm                - Return KaeKae to normal, chill temperament
  !tone funny          - Set KaeKae tone to funny (Only allowed)
  !tone happy          - Set KaeKae tone to happy (Only allowed)
  !diss [username]     - Aggressively witty roast of target or random user

[User Intelligence & Lookups]
  who is <username>    - Detailed narrative biography from audio & text files
  info on <username>   - Statistical summary (msg count, mic airtime, dates, tone)

[Moderation Audit Queries]
  who blocked [user]   - List who blocked target (ignores unblocks)
  who unblocked [user] - List who unblocked target (ignores blocks)
  who kicked [user]    - List who kicked target
  who punished [user]  - List who punished target (ignores unpunished)
  who unpunished [user]- List who unpunished target
  who banned [user]    - List who banned target (ignores unbanned)
  who unbanned [user]  - List who unbanned target

[Speech Memory & Verbatim]
  !verbatim <username> - Send 5 most recent mic transcripts for user
  !verbatimall         - Send 5 most recent mic transcripts from everyone
  !recall "phrase"     - Search mic speech archives with exact/AND matching
  !idk <question>      - Ask anything (Web Search + Memory + Room context)
  !mo                  - Page through next chunk of search/verbatim results

[Voice Triggers (Spoken on Mic)]
  'kae', 'kaekae', 'kaebot'
  -> Random non-repeating replies:
     "What's up, Doc" | "What you need, homie?" | "Why do I keep hearing my name being called?"

[Authorized Creator Only]
  !say "message"       - Have KaeKae speak/type message in her own tone
